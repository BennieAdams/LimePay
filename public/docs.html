What it does

Receives USDC payments from senders, deducts a configurable platform fee (0–5% in basis points), routes the net amount to the recipient, and sends the fee to a treasury address. Includes emergency pause/unpause (owner only), two-step ownership transfer (Ownable2Step), full reentrancy protection, and renounceOwnership is permanently disabled to prevent lockout.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about LimePay.

Do I need crypto to use LimePay?
You need a small amount of USDC on Arc Testnet for gas fees (since Arc uses USDC as its native gas token). You can get free test USDC from the "Get test USDC" button inside the app or from the Arc Testnet faucet. No ETH or other gas tokens are ever needed.
How does sending by username work?
When you register on LimePay, you get a unique @username (e.g. @john_doe) and an LP-account number (e.g. LP-12345678). Other users can type your username or account number in the Send page to resolve it to your wallet address automatically — no copying and pasting of 42-character hex addresses.
Is LimePay non-custodial?
Yes. LimePay never holds your funds. You connect your own wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, etc.) and sign every transaction yourself. LimePay's smart contract only routes payments — it cannot move funds without your wallet approval.
Which chains does the bridge support?
LimePay's Bridge uses Circle's CCTP protocol and supports: Arc Testnet, Ethereum Sepolia, Base Sepolia, Arbitrum Sepolia, Polygon Amoy, Avalanche Fuji, and Solana Devnet — 7 chains in total.
Are the Earn and Borrow rates real?
On testnet the rates are indicative (USDC 5.2% APY, EURC 4.8%, cirBTC 3.1%). They represent what the protocol targets for mainnet deployment using Circle Yield and Arc Lending. Actual mainnet rates will depend on market conditions.
Is the smart contract audited?
Yes. The LimePayPaymentRouter was reviewed for security issues including reentrancy, ownership attacks, and pause lockouts. All findings were fixed before deployment. Slither static analysis returned 0 findings and 49/49 forge unit tests pass including fuzz and invariant tests. Note: this is a testnet contract — an independent third-party audit is recommended before mainnet deployment.
How do I get test USDC?
Inside the LimePay app, click "Get test USDC" in the sidebar. This drips ERC-20 USDC directly to your connected wallet address on Arc Testnet — free, instant, no external faucet needed.

Ready to try LimePay?
